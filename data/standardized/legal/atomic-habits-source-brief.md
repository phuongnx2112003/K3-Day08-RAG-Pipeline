# atomic-habits-source-brief

Atomic Habits - Source Brief

This team-written brief summarizes a public article by James Clear.
The article presents a four-step habit loop: cue, craving, response, and reward.
For building a good habit, its four practical laws are: make it obvious, attractive,
easy, and satisfying.
The brief is a paraphrase for retrieval practice and is not a replacement for the book.
