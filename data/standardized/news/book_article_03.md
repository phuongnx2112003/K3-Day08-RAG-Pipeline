System 1 and System 2 Thinking

What is System 1 and System 2 Thinking?

System 1 and System 2 thinking

describes two distinct modes of cognitive processing introduced by

Daniel Kahneman

in his book

Thinking, Fast and Slow.

System 1

is fast, automatic, and intuitive, operating with little to no effort. This mode of thinking allows us to make quick decisions
