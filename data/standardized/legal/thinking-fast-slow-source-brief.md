# thinking-fast-slow-source-brief

Thinking Fast and Slow - Source Brief

This team-written brief summarizes a public reference about ideas popularized by Daniel
Kahneman.
System 1 is fast, automatic, and intuitive, which makes everyday judgments efficient.
System 2 is slower, deliberate, and effortful, and is used for analytical or complex
problems.
Neither system is universally better; context and awareness of bias matter for
decisions.
