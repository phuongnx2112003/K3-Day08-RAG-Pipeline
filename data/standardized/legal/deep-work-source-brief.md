# deep-work-source-brief

Deep Work - Source Brief

This team-written brief summarizes Cal Newport's public description of Deep Work.
Deep work means focusing without distraction on a cognitively demanding task.
The source explains that focused practice supports learning difficult skills and
producing high-value output.
The book overview presents practical strategies such as time blocking and depth rituals.
